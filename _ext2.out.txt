Meanwhile, the two groups execute completely different position update formulas to ensure the efficiency of the grouping operation and assign distinct search tasks to each group. During iterations, the group sizes are not static but are dynamically adjusted after each iteration based on changes in the fitness value of the optimal solution. Under normal conditions, DMSGWO gradually reduces the exploration group size while increasing the exploitation group size to enhance local search capability and improve convergence accuracy.
